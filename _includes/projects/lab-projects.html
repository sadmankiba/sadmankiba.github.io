Taxonomy of Construction Task Activities

IROS 2026 Future of Construction Workshop

July 2026 - Sep 2026

Developed a taxonomy of construction task activities by analyzing O*NET data and instructional construction task videos. The taxonomy identifies primitive actions for a robot worker to perform construction tasks. The primitives can be composed into skills and in different combinations to facilitate complex construction tasks. [Arxiv] [PDF] [Data] [Demo]

Wrist Design for Tendon-Driven Hands

IROS 2026 Sensors and Actuators for Dexterous Manipulation Workshop

Jun 2026 - Sep 2026

We designed a 2-DoF wrist extension for a tendon-driven hand (CRAFT hand) that does not require any modifications to the existing hand structure. Our concentric design allows for a compact wrist with minimal axial depth. [Arxiv] [PDF] [Website]

Near-ground Target Search with UAV

In Review

Oct 2025 - Sep 2026

Proposed a balanced exploration-exploitation strategy in both horizontal and vertical directions for a UAV to search for a target near the ground. The UAV follows a set route and opportunistically explores when it gets stuck to find flight corridors avoiding large and wide obstacles.
