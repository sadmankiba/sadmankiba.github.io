Memory-efficient Low-Rank Training for Large Foundation Models

Course Project

Mar 2024 - May 2024

LoRA adaptation reduces the number of trainable parameters in fine-tuning. Recent works have also extended LoRA for pre-training large models. In this project, we evaluated the performance of two recent LoRA-inspired techniques (LTE and GaLore) on large language and vision models. Our experiments show that parallel LoRA with a small rank can achieve performance close to full-parameter training, ViTs can converge faster and achieve higher accuracy with LoRA adaptation and LoRA is most effective in the attention layers of a Transformer model. Explaining Delayed Generalization (Grokking) of Overparameterized Models

Course Project

Mar 2024 - May 2024

Grokking is the delayed generalization phenomena observed when training a small dataset with an overparameterized model. In this project, we reproduced grokking in Transformer and MLP models, and explained the relationship of hyperparameters and model parameters with grokking. Our results establish the strong effect of training data size and batch size to grokking delays, and demonstrate the empirical properties of loss contour and weight norm in grokking. [Code]

Distributed Hash Join On Remote Shared Memory

Research Project

Jan 2024 – Aug 2024

Building hash table on distributed nodes can consume a significant amount of memory. In this project, we explore the feasibility of building a hash table on remote shared memory. We implemented a distributed hash join algorithm with Actor framework and Arrow on top of a linear-probing hash table. [Code]
