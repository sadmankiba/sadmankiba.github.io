Hi! I am Sadman. I am a first-year PhD student in the Center for Resilient Autonomous Systems (RISE) at UCI, advised by Professor Mohammad Abdullah Al Faruque. I got my Masters from University of Wisconsin-Madison in 2024. My research focus is in autonomous systems and AI for robotics. I am currently working on adapting vision-language-action models for planning and reasoning in autonomous systems and robotics. . In my recent projects, I have worked on multi-modal models, text analysis and generation, image analysis and generation, and state-space models.

Before starting my PhD, I worked briefly at Meta focusing on networking for AI training/inferenece. I also had the opporunity to intern at HPE in Summer 2023 and at Meta in Summer 2024, working on AI/HPC systems. Before coming to UW-Madison, I obtained my undergraduate major in Computer Science from Bangladesh University of Engineering and Technology and my undergraduate thesis was on Interpretable CNN .
